## Part 1 — Held weapons

### The problem
Weapons are a separate PNG drawn at a guessed hand spot. They don't follow the hand, nothing
covers the grip, and they don't turn with the arm, so they read as stickers. The current
weaponless clips are stiff and the weapon arc isn't synced to the arm.

### The fix
1. **Know where the hand is on every frame.** Remake the weaponless clips with custom
   skeleton-v3 poses (we write `right_hand` / `right_elbow` per frame), so the hand point and
   forearm angle are known. Save as `art/<body>/Bare_<Anim>.hand.json` (`[x, y, angle]` per frame).
2. **Grip point per weapon.** A `GRIP` table: where the handle is on each weapon PNG and its
   resting angle. The grip sits exactly on the hand point, turned by the forearm angle.
3. **Fingers over the handle.** Redraw a small patch of the hand *after* the weapon so the fist
   wraps the grip. In the back view the weapon goes *behind* the body.
4. **Swing synced to the pose.** Wind-up frames hold the weapon back, strike frames sweep the
   arc, follow-through settles. Timing from the frame index, not a fixed 0.3s.
5. **Projectiles leave the hand.** Thrown/shot items start at the tracked hand point (visual
   only; the hit box doesn't change).

### Must-haves (from you)
- **Kunai are not a "brick of three."** New single-kunai art (and single shuriken, single caltrop).
  Hold one or two between the fingers, fanned like a real thrower, and throw them one at a time
  to match the triple throw.
- **Every weapon is actually held.** No weapon floats next to the hand or sits as a flat PNG.
- **Correct angles per weapon.** Blades edge-forward, axe head forward, spear shaft along the
  forearm, fan closed at rest and opened flat when swung, shield face out, bow upright,
  crossbow level at the shoulder, blowgun at the mouth. Checked in side, front and back views.
- **An animation that fits each weapon:**

| Weapon | Motion |
|---|---|
| Ōdachi, axe | heavy two-hand swing |
| War fan, jutte, twin sai | quick one-hand strikes |
| Flame spear | thrust |
| Shield | bash, and a raised guard |
| Kunai, shuriken | flick throw |
| Smoke bomb, caltrops | underhand toss |
| Kusarigama | chain spin, then throw |
| Bow | draw to the cheek, release |
| Crossbow | raise to shoulder, fire |
| Blowgun | raise to mouth, puff |

Every clip is reviewed frame by frame before import; anything that looks off is redone.

### New clips per body (PixelLab)
Heavy two-hand swing, quick strike, thrust, bash/guard, flick throw, underhand toss, chain spin,
bow draw, crossbow aim, blowgun puff, plus idle/run/up/down with hand data. Samurai first
(~40 gens), check in the preview, then the other 5 bodies (~120 gens).

Also try PixelLab `edit_animation` on one clip ("holding a kunai in the right hand") to compare a
weapon baked into the art against the overlay before committing.

## Part 2 — Unique ultimates

### How it will work
- **Cutscenes:** today every class shares a few poses and background "motifs," so many look the
  same. Each of the 16 classes gets its own short scene script (`src/cutscenes/<class>.ts`):
  its own props, camera, timing and finishing beat, drawn around the character's pixel art.
- **Props as pixel art:** one or two PixelLab object batches (16 items each): half-open war fan,
  conch shell, clan banner, taiko drum, prayer wheel, dice cup and dice, wooden clappers, ink
  brush, paper talisman, red debt tag, ice crystal, sumo salt bowl, kappa head dish, kumadori
  face mark, coins, single kunai.
- **In-fight effects** live in `src/ultvfx.ts`, and every one reads the ult's real remaining time,
  so no effect ends early (the Onmyoji bug) or hangs around after.

### Your choices, class by class

**Shinobi — Shadow Step** (4s vanish)
- Cutscene: close-up on the mask, a kunai flicked at the camera, then a puff of smoke and only glowing eyes are left.
- Fight: you become a dark silhouette with faint afterimages trailing behind.

**Ronin — Crimson Debt** (brand 3 foes, collected 1.5s later)
- Cutscene: the ronin draws the blade across their palm; blood drips into three red seals.
- Fight: each target gets a hanging red debt tag that counts down 3, 2, 1.

**Yamabushi — Mountain's Endurance** (3s immunity)
- Cutscene: blows a conch shell (horagai) as a mountain silhouette rises behind.
- Fight: stone-skin cracks glow gold as hits are soaked up.

**Arashi — Storm of Heaven** (2.5s arrow rain)
- Cutscene: bow drawn straight up, clouds roll in and lightning lights the archer.
- Fight: arrows fall as streaks mixed with rain, with splashes where they land.

**Hatamoto — Shogun's Decree** (8s banner)
- Cutscene: the banner unfurls with the clan crest as war drums hit.
- Fight: a golden dome around the banner, edges rippling like cloth.

**Kunoichi — Phantom Dance**
- Cutscene: a fan opens and hides **half** her face; three silhouettes step out in different
  colours. She and the silhouettes **glitch** (slice offsets, colour split, skipped frames)
  because she moves so fast.
- Fight: petals and ribbon trails follow each afterimage.

**Kagebushi — Shadow Consume** (6s, kills extend it)
- Cutscene: a pool of darkness spreads from the feet and swallows the body; red eyes open.
- Fight: shadow tendrils whip out on each hit; the screen edges darken, pulsing a little more
  with each kill that extends it.

**Raijin — Heaven's Drum** (6s)
- Cutscene: a ring of taiko drums appears behind and gets struck one by one.
- Fight: your weapon crackles blue the whole 6s.

**Yuki-onna — Eternal Winter** (6s blizzard)
- Cutscene: she breathes frost across the screen; her kimono turns to ice.
- Fight: frozen foes sit inside an ice-crystal shell that shatters on death.

**Sohei — Prayer Wheel** (4s)
- Cutscene: a bronze prayer wheel spins with sutra text lighting up.
- Fight: each pulse is a golden ring of written prayers pushing outward.

**Kappa — Tidal Surge**
- Cutscene: the kappa bows, the water in its head dish spills and becomes a river.
- Fight: a real wave crest with foam and fish, carrying enemies on top; puddles stay where the
  wave dropped foes (shows the slow).

**Kabuki — Grand Finale** (5s crits)
- Cutscene: the striped kabuki curtain pulls open, wooden clappers crack, a cross-eyed mie pose.
- Fight: crits flash a red-and-white kumadori face-paint mark; the spotlight cone follows you
  and dims as the 5s run out.

**Bakuto — Roll of Fate**
- Cutscene: the dice cup slams on a tatami mat; dice tumble out showing your numbers.
- Fight: the two dice float above you showing the result while it lasts; a jackpot rains coins;
  a low roll cracks the dice in two.

**Rikishi — Dohyō Stomp** (3 stomps)
- Cutscene: salt thrown in the air, then the high leg-raise stomp (shiko).
- Fight: the ring's straw rope glows and the ground cracks on each stomp; dust shockwaves with a
  big 1, 2, 3 count.

**Sumi-e Painter — Living Ink** (6s tiger)
- Cutscene: brush strokes paint a tiger across the screen, then it blinks and leaps out.
- Fight: the tiger is drawn as live brush strokes that wobble and drip.

**Onmyoji — Spirit Tempest** (5s vortex)
- Cutscene: a five-point seal (seimei) draws itself in light; paper talismans fly out.
- Fight: a ring of paper talismans orbits the vortex and burns away as the 5s count down.
- Bug: the visuals must last the full 5s (step 0).
